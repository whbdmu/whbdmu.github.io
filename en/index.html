Biography

Huibing Wang is a Professor with the School of Information Science and Technology, Dalian Maritime University, China. He received the Ph.D. degree in computer applied technology from Dalian University of Technology in 2018, and was a joint Ph.D. student at the University of Adelaide, Australia (2016–2017). His research focuses on computer vision, multimedia analysis, multi-view learning, and intelligent perception for underwater robotics. He has published 60+ papers in top-tier venues such as IEEE TMM, TIFS, IJCV, CVPR, ICCV, NeurIPS, ICML, AAAI, IJCAI, and ACM MM, with ten ESI Highly Cited Papers (four ESI Hot Papers). He was listed among the World's Top 2% Scientists (Stanford University & Elsevier) for three consecutive years (2024–2026). He is a Leading Talent of the Ministry of Transport of China and a recipient of the Liaoning Provincial Excellent Young Scientists Fund. He serves on the editorial boards of IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology, Pattern Recognition, and other journals.

Research Interests
Computer Vision Multimedia Analysis Multi-view Learning Person Re-Identification & Search Domain Adaptation Underwater Robotics Foundation Models
  • Computer vision & multimedia analysis — person re-identification and person search, unsupervised domain adaptation, event-based visual tracking, and adversarial robustness.
  • Multi-view learning — incomplete multi-view clustering, anchor-graph learning, and tensor completion for heterogeneous multimodal data.
  • Intelligent underwater systems — visual perception, 3D panoramic reconstruction with missing modalities, hyperspectral analysis, and underwater robots for marine ranching.
